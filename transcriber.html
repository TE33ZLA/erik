With headphones (choose “Zoom audio + my mic”):
  1. Join Zoom in Chrome: on the Zoom join page click “Join from your browser”.
    Windows users can also keep the Zoom app and share the Entire screen instead.
  2. Press Start. The first time, it downloads the speech model (about 1 minute).
  3. A sharing window opens. Pick the Zoom tab (or Entire screen) and switch on “Share tab audio” / “Share system audio”. Click Share.
  4. Allow the microphone, so your own voice is included.
  5. Watch the Health check box. Green means it is working.
  6. Press Stop at the end. The transcript saves as a .txt file automatically.
“Zoom audio” mode turns speech into text on this computer. Nothing is uploaded. “Microphone only” mode uses Chrome's built-in recognition, which sends audio to Google.
